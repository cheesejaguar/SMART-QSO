# Governance
- **Maintainers:** the principal investigator (PI, licensee KK6GDB) is today the only maintainer and the only approver (`docs/project/CONFIGURATION_MANAGEMENT_PLAN.md` §2.2).
- **Review:** a change to a baseline (Class I: baseline parameters, requirements, ICDs) needs a change request in `docs/project/CHANGE_REQUEST_LOG.md` and the PI's approval; once a second contributor exists, at least one reviewer who is not the author reviews the pull request (CMP §2.2).
- **Decision‑making:** design decisions are the PI's and are recorded in `docs/BASELINE_PARAMETERS.md` §9. Proposals from design reviews (for example the chair decisions of `docs/reviews/CDR/CDR_2026_DESIGN_REVIEW.md` §4) bind nothing until the PI ratifies them.
- **Releases:** Semantic versioning for software; dated drops for docs/hardware.
