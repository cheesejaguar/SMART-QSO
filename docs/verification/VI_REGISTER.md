## 1. Purpose

The design documents define their own verification items (VI-…, V-…, AV-V…): a test, inspection or analysis with a
pass criterion and a time, usually closing a review item discrepancy (RID) of the CDR 2026 design review. Before this
register they sat in 20 documents and none was in the VCRM (RID P3P-01). This register lists every one of them with
its planned event and date from the critical-path schedule, its owner, its parent RID and its status, and adds the
decision points and dated actions of `mission/SCHEDULE.md` §5. The VCRM stays the record of requirement verification;
this register is the record of the items that feed it.

## 2. Rules

1. An item is defined once, in the table of the document that owns it, whose first cell is the item's ID. Its test and
   pass criterion are edited there, never here: `python3 scripts/gen_vi_register.py` regenerates §3.
2. The event and date come from the item's "when" cell, matched to the events of the critical-path model
   (`software/simulation/cdr/pm/schedule_cpm.py`): a written date is used as written, L-relative dates count from the
   planning launch (2028-11-01), and the earliest event named is the item's first event. The dates move when the
   schedule moves.
3. Where the defining document lacks a field (no timing, no number, a test on the wrong article), the generator's
   `OVERRIDES` table supplies it with its reason, shown in italics in the criterion. The defining document's owner
   carries the override into the next revision of that document, and the override is then removed.
4. An item is Open until committed evidence closes it (a test report, analysis or inspection record cited in its row),
   as in the VCRM.
5. CI fails (`--check`) if this file is stale, an item has no pass criterion or no dated event, an ID is defined twice in
   one document, or a decision point or dated action has no owner, no date or no criterion. An ID defined in two
   documents is listed once, with both sources; the two definitions must agree.
6. Owners are roles; only the PI is named today (PROJECT_PLAN §4), so every other role is "(PI until named)".
