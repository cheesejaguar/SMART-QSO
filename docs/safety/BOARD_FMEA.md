## 1. Purpose, Scope and Basis

SQSO-FMEA-002 C analysed the spacecraft at the function level from BASELINE Rev D and the block schematics S1–S8. The
closure panel found that it says nothing about the circuits that round 2 designed to build-to level: the window-watchdog
supervisor, the receiver lines that gate the beacon-continues rule (REL-04), the mission clock, the hardware load shed,
the AntS bus buffer, the four burn lines and their one-shots, the deployment-switch inhibits, the end-of-life arm and
fire lines, the recharge lockout, the pack and tap fuses, the magnetometer board A2, the access-port board A3, the
transponder heartbeat lease, the Jetson carrier's eFuse, sequencing and translators, and the RF board's TNC health
monostable, carrier detect and PA. This document is that analysis.

## 2. Method

- **Form.** MIL-STD-1629A task 101 (functional and interface FMEA) and task 102 by category, tailored for Class D under
  NPR 8705.4B, as in SQSO-FMEA-002 §1. One row per failure mode of a circuit function or interface: item, function,
  failure-mode class (open, short, stuck, drift, loss of output, spurious), cause, local effect, system and mission
  effect, detection (telemetry, flight software, hardware, ground test), compensating provision, severity, criticality,
  single-point flag, references (CIL item, SQSO-FMEA-002 row, finding, fix).
- **Severity** uses the SQSO-FMEA-002 categories: **1** catastrophic or critical hazard (SQSO-SDP-002); **2** single
  failure after which no further success level can be met; **3** loses a success criterion or degrades service, the mission
  continues; **4** negligible. **Criticality** is the category with **R** where a redundant element or working
  alternate remains: 2R as in SQSO-FMEA-002, and **1R** for a Category 1 mode whose hazard keeps at least two further
  independent controls (the counts of SQSO-BSA-001 and SQSO-SDP-002). **SPF**: **Y** a single failure that ends the
  mission (severity 2, criticality 2); **L2** a single point for transponder service (Level 2) only; a dash otherwise.
- **Rules applied.** (1) Sunlit-only operation (pack isolated, bus from the array through EPS §5.2) is Category 2: the
  beacon may continue in sunlight, but L1-03, L2 and L3 can no longer be met. (2) A latent failure is rated by its own
  effect, with the combined effect stated in the row (for example a receiver-evidence line that reads healthy). (3) The
  flight software's response is taken from SRS 1.8 and the named Rust module; where it sets the outcome the row says so.
  (4) Every path into an unpowered or isolated domain was checked against the netlists, not only the design text.
- **Rows and counts.** The rows are held in `docs/safety/BOARD_FMEA_ROWS.csv`; `python3 scripts/board_fmea.py` checks
  them (IDs, categories, flags, no open-item words) and writes §3's counts and §6's tables; `--check` fails if this
  document is stale.

### 4.1 Secondary protector and F3 moved to the battery sense board (EPS F, BATTERY_DESIGN 2.9)

The EPS designer moved the BQ771806 (U2), its heater FET Q16, the zener and F3 (ITV4030, rated −10 … +65 °C) onto the
battery sense board, with U2's own taps at the cell tabs, its own supply (BAT_TOP after F0B) and its own ground (cell-1
negative), and argues that F3 has to blow only during an overcharge, which needs charge current, which the hardware
refuses below +1.1 °C. F1 stays on the EPS and is fired only with the EPS board ≥ 0 °C and in sunlight (ICD-001 1.6
§4.6). Independent check:

| Question | Assessment | Rows, RID |
|----------|------------|-----------|

## 5. Findings and Proposed Design Changes

Severity is the category of the worst row now, and with the proposed change. Each change keeps the three inhibits per
source and every hazard control count unless the row says otherwise. **Not accepted as designed** (a change is required before the EM
build, or before flight for SMA-B02): SMA-B02 to SMA-B14, SMA-B17, SMA-B19, SMA-B20 to SMA-B22. **Recommended**, retained
in the CIL if not taken: SMA-B01, SMA-B15, SMA-B16, SMA-B18, SMA-B24; SMA-B23 is editorial (SQSO-FMEA-002 D §4).
Status in Version B: SMA-B03 taken (EPS E tap ladder; EPS F separate U2 taps); the EPS and flight-software designers have
answered several others in EPS E/F and SRS 1.9, not re-assessed here except where §4.1 says so.

## 8. Limits of This Analysis

- Behaviour of parts in failure is from their data sheets and design notes; where a data sheet does not state it (the
  TPS3850 with CWD shorted, a TPS55340 with its output diode shorted), the row takes the worse credible outcome and a
  V-BFM test settles it.
- The RF board's netlist was not parsed (its KiCad file differs in form from the others); its rows rest on the design
  text and the BOM. The EPS, OBC, A2 and A3 rows were checked against the generated net lists.
- No failure rates are assigned; criticality is by category. The reliability model (`docs/environment/RELIABILITY_ANALYSIS.md`
  v2.1) carries the rates of the single-point chains.
- The four EPS changes are analysed as drafted on 2026-10-27; if the EPS design moves again, §4 and the Rev D rows are
  re-run.
