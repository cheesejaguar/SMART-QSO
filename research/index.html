Synthetic experiment · host-tested · no flight benefit established.

The conventional baseline uses deterministic fair relay scheduling. A compact predictor can defer attempts when contact is unlikely. Both policies use the same production C relay and deterministic resource gate, matched message arrivals and channel conditions. Neither can bypass spacecraft safety limits.

A matched, held-out comparison

The model trains on earlier synthetic records and evaluates a later window using 30matched seeds. Scenarios include eclipse, solar uncertainty, degraded batteries, packet loss and collisions, heavy traffic and an eight-hour contact outage.

What this does not prove

  • Visibility is synthetic phase windows, not mission ephemerides
  • Power, collision/loss and compute energy are unmeasured inputs
  • One shared downlink recipient; geographic exposure not proven
  • No accepted flight model, device timing or environmental evidence

The compute overhead is an assumed 0.01 J per decision. RF and contact profiles are synthetic. The interval describes variation across these seeds; it does not capture uncertainty in real hardware, orbital access or the chosen environment. The predictor is not selected or enabled in flight firmware.
