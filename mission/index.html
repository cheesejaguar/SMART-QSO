The mission

SMART-QSO would ride a SpaceX Transporter rideshare to a 475 km sun-synchronous orbit, serve amateur radio operators for 2 years, then passivate and decay. This page follows it from the dispenser to re-entry. It is a plan: no launch is contracted and nothing has been built. Concept of operations

From the dispenser to re-entry

The concept of operations in plain language. Times after separation (S) come from the mission simulation and the pass analysis. Mission timeline §3–§7

  1. L − 47 days

    Into the dispenser

    Final charge to 60 % at the integrator, key load, then into the EXOpod Nova. Unpowered from here: three deployment switches hold every circuit open, and nobody touches the spacecraft again. SQSO-BL-001 §4

  2. Launch

    Falcon 9 from Vandenberg

    A Transporter-class rideshare to sun-synchronous orbit; the dispenser opens on the integrator's signal. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.1

  3. S

    Separation

    The switches close and the flight computer boots. The 30-minute deployment wait and the 45-minute radio silence start counting. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.2

  4. S + 120 s

    Detumble starts

    Nothing moves for the first 120 s (a launch-provider rule). Then the magnetorquers start slowing the spacecraft's tumble. SQSO-BL-001 §4

  5. S + 30 min

    Wings, then antennas

    Burn resistors release the two solar wings, one resistor at a time. The antenna unit deploys next, but only once it reads −17 °C or warmer; after a cold eclipse separation it waits for the next 50-minute slot. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.2, C-18

  6. S + 45 min

    First beacon

    The radio silence ends and is latched. The receiver comes on and the first beacon goes out, in IDLE mode with template text every 120 s, once the antennas are out. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.2

  7. S + ≈ 1 h

    Sun pointing

    Detumbled, the spacecraft spins at 8°/s about the axis that faces its wings to the Sun. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.2

  8. S + 0.7–13.7 h

    First contact

    The first pass over the San Francisco Bay Area comes 5.3 h after separation in the median case; the first commanding pass 6.4 h after it (at most 13.7 h). SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.2

  9. Days 1–14

    Commissioning

    Authenticated telecommand checked from each station, TX-OFF proven, power and thermal characterised. Only then are the transponder and the Jetson released. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.2

  10. 2 years

    Operations

    The transponder opens only in sunlight, with the battery at 55 % or more, for at most 25 % of an orbit. The beacon runs every 60 to 180 s depending on the power mode. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.2

  11. End of mission

    Passivation

    A two-step authenticated command permanently disconnects the battery charge path while the transmitter still reports, sends a final frame, then latches the transmitter off. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.2

  12. ≤ 5 years after

    Re-entry

    Natural decay with the wings deployed, within the FCC's five-year rule. There is no propulsion and no separate drag device. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.2

Operating modes

The flight computer moves between modes on the battery's state of charge, sunlight, temperature and the watchdog; faults drop to SAFE, and TX-OFF and the loss-of-contact inhibit override every mode. CONOPS.md §3

  • Launch‑Safe: inhibits armed; radio unpowered; awaiting deployer release
  • Commissioning hold: from separation until released by an authenticated command; IDLE at most, no transponder, no Jetson
  • Boot/Detumble: attitude control to reduce rates; minimal housekeeping; beacon only after the RF silence
  • SAFE: lowest power; template beacon every 180 s; no transponder, no Jetson
  • IDLE: beacon every 120 s; Jetson suspended; transponder only if the power gates allow
  • ACTIVE: beacon every 60 s; transponder windows and Jetson bursts permitted within the gates of §5
  • EMERGENCY: below 15 % SOC; beacon shed; receiver stays on

Fail-graceful by design

The Jetson is power-gated and used only in short bursts in sunlight. If it is off, faulty, or its text fails the content filter, the beacon uses a template and the transponder runs on fixed rules. CONOPS.md §5
