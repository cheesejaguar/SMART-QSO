Amateur radio CubeSat · design phase

SMART-QSO

A 1U CubeSat that will carry a single-channel FM transponder for amateur radio contacts worldwide, switched on only when the power budget allows, and a 1200 bps AX.25 telemetry beacon. Its experiment is onboard AI that schedules the transponder within the power budget and chooses the beacon's plain-language text. If the AI is off or fails, it is a conventional transponder with template beacons. SQSO-BL-001 §2

Where the project stands

  • Design phase. The project is in Phase B (pre-PDR). No hardware has been bought or built, and every figure is a prediction, none a measurement. SQSO-BL-001 §1 SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §7.2
  • Software runs in simulation. The flight software (Rust, on the Embassy executor) runs as a host simulation and builds as an STM32L4R5ZI image that CI checks; it has not run on hardware. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §7.2
  • Reviewed, but not by an independent board. A design review against the NASA CDR criteria finished on 2026-10-27. It was carried out by AI reviewers, not an independent review board, and it rates the project at the level of a strong PDR, not past a CDR. The independent PDR is planned for 2027-01-12. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §1.2, §7.2
  • No licence, no frequencies, no launch contract yet. No regulatory filing has been made; the IARU coordination request is not submitted, so there are no coordinated frequencies. The launch contract is due by 2027-06-30. SQSO-REG-004 SQSO-SCH-001 §1

What the AI does, and what it does not

Flight baseline: a selector on the flight computer

A small calibrated model on the STM32L4R5 flight computer picks among pre-approved beacon templates and proposes the power mode and the transponder schedule. Hard limits stay outside the model: no AI output can override the power gates, the transmit inhibits or TX-OFF. It is designed, not yet implemented; today the flight software sends its fixed templates in turn. SQSO-BL-001 §6 CONOPS.md §5

Gated experiment: generated text on the Jetson

A power-gated NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano module can generate beacon lines with a small open language model (SmolLM2-360M or Qwen3-0.6B) in bursts of at most 10 minutes. It flies as an experiment only if the energy per accepted beacon line, measured on the engineering model, is at most 0.1 Wh. Generated text carries no numbers and passes the same content filter as the templates; the flight computer writes every value from measurements. SQSO-BL-001 §6

How it reaches orbit

The planned path, as the design review traced it. None of it is contracted yet: requests for quotes go out by 2026-12-31. SQSO-SCH-001 §1

  1. SpaceX Falcon 9

    A Transporter-class rideshare to sun-synchronous orbit from Vandenberg SLC-4E. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.1

  2. Exolaunch

    The integrator: SpaceX's customer. SMART-QSO would contract with the integrator, not with SpaceX. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.1

  3. EXOpod Nova

    The dispenser. The CubeSat is loaded about 47 days before launch, unpowered, and is not touched again. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.1

  4. 475 km SSO

    Drop-off at a 475 km target, never above 485 km, a limit the contract must state. SQSO-REV-CDR-002 §3.1

  5. Re-entry

    No propulsion: the orbit decays naturally with the wings deployed, within 5 years of the end of the mission. SQSO-BL-001 §3
