Service first. Experiment second.

Messages remain readable and unchanged. The optional computer cannot command the spacecraft or replace factual telemetry.

What happens when the payload fails?

The MCU disables payload power, retains the relay queue and uses a deterministic status template. The failure is recorded for the ground operator.

How are commands protected?

Ed25519 signatures authenticate telecommands. A persistent sequence floor rejects replay. Radio shutdown remains inhibited after a reset.

What is still unproven?

3U feasibility, measured payload energy, vacuum cooling, radiation tolerance, RF performance, hardware inhibits and disposal all require external verification.

Evidence before orbit.

A repository-stage mission, developed openly. Software evidence and physical qualification are tracked separately.
